02

The strategy, in two minutes

Your hour per case

  1. 15 min 5 MCQs. 3 min each: 2 to think and look up, 1 to mark.
  2. 45 min Descriptives. 15 min per 5 marks: 10 to think and find, 5 to write.
  3. At 60 Move to the next case. Always.

The first 15 minutes

  1. Read only the questions of all 5 cases.
  2. Next to each, write the subject and chapter.
  3. Drop the case with the most "not sure" questions.
  4. Start with your strongest case.

Law and Costing

Don't try to finish them now. Learn to name the chapter from the question's words, then let the index take you to the page.

The solving flow, for every question

Not coming in time? Leave space, move on. Come back at the end.
Know it already? Write it without the book. That's where you save time.
A practical? Find the similar illustration in the SM and replace the figures.
Never leave it blank. Provision, facts, conclusion: something relevant always scores.
