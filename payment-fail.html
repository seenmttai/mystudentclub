Did your bank deduct money from your account?

If money was deducted from your UPI or card during this attempt, it is held securely in the banking network. Most banks automatically reverse failed transactions within 3 to 5 business days.

Need urgent manual activation? If you have been charged, you do not need to pay twice. Simply message our WhatsApp support team with your transaction screenshot, and we will verify and manually activate your LMS course within minutes.

Common Reasons for Payment Failure
Bank Server Timeout

Your bank or UPI app took too long to authorize the transaction, causing a session timeout.

Account Limits Reached

Daily UPI transaction limits or card online payment restrictions might have blocked authorization.

Verification Mismatch

Incorrect UPI PIN entered or OTP expired before completion.

Network Drop

Internet connectivity was interrupted while returning from the bank authorization page.
