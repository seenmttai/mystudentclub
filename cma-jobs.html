How CMA practical training works

  • ICMAI calls it practical training. Students who registered for Intermediate on or after 11 February 2020 need 15 months of full-time training (35 hours a week).
  • You can start as soon as you register for CMA Intermediate. You do not need to pass Intermediate first.
  • At least 10 months must be certified on Form T-5 before you sit the CMA Final exam (by 31 August for December, by 28/29 February for June).
  • Train under a Practising Cost Accountant or a firm of Cost Accountants (Form T-1, with UDIN), or in a recognised organisation (Form T-4): government bodies, PSUs, banks, insurers, universities, MSMEs and start-ups, or companies above ICMAI's turnover limit.
  • Articleship registered with ICAI or ICSI does not count for CMA training.
  • Minimum stipend: ₹2,000 a month in year one under a Practising Cost Accountant. In companies (from 1 August 2024), ₹15,000 to ₹20,000 a month in year one for Intermediate students and ₹20,000 to ₹25,000 for Final students, depending on the city.
  • Report your training to ICMAI within 30 days of joining. You can change your trainer at most three times.

Summary as of October 2026. Sources: ICMAI: Practical Training Scheme 2020 · ICMAI: practical training FAQ. Rules change, so confirm on the ICMAI website.

How we pick listings

We show vacancies whose description says CMA (ICMAI) candidates can apply (for example "CA/CMA", "CMA Inter", "ICWA" or "Cost Accountant"). We try to leave out US CMA roles (Certified Management Accountant, from the US-based IMA), "CMA data" (a bank loan report, not the qualification), and listings that say CMA candidates are not eligible or only want CMA on top of a mandatory CA. Each listing quotes the line that matched so you can judge for yourself.

Frequently asked questions

Is CMA articleship the same as CMA practical training?

Yes. ICMAI officially calls it practical training under the Practical Training Scheme 2020, and the trainee is a Cost & Management Trainee. Students commonly say CMA articleship or CMA internship. Source

How long is CMA practical training and when can I start?

15 months, for students who registered for Intermediate on or after 11 February 2020. You can start right after registering for CMA Intermediate. Students on the older scheme need three years. Source

Can I do CMA training in a CA or CS firm?

CA or CS articleship is not recognised for CMA training. Training counts under a Practising Cost Accountant or firm of Cost Accountants, or in a recognised organisation. Work in a CA or CS firm's separate management consultancy division gets only partial credit, so ask who will sign your Form T-1 or T-4. Source

What is the minimum stipend for CMA trainees?

Under a Practising Cost Accountant: ₹2,000, ₹3,000 and ₹4,000 a month in years one, two and three. In companies and other organisations (from 1 August 2024): ₹15,000 to ₹20,000 a month in year one for Intermediate students and ₹20,000 to ₹25,000 for Final students, depending on the city category. Source

Is there a separate industrial training phase in CMA?

No. Unlike CA, CMA has no separate industrial phase: all 15 months can be done in a recognised organisation (Form T-4), such as a company, bank or PSU. Some PSUs also run Industrial Trainee (Finance) schemes for CA / CMA Inter passed candidates, usually with an age limit, so watch their notifications. Source

A job mentions "CMA data". Is it meant for CMA students?

Not necessarily. CMA data is the Credit Monitoring Arrangement report banks use for working-capital loans. It has nothing to do with the CMA qualification, so we do not treat it as a CMA listing. Source

Is CMA India the same as US CMA?

No. CMA India is ICMAI's statutory qualification and carries rights such as cost audit. US CMA (Certified Management Accountant) is a separate credential from the US-based Institute of Management Accountants (IMA). We try to leave out listings that ask only for US CMA. Source
