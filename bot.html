MSC-JobsBot

MSC-JobsBot is the job-listing crawler of My Student Club, a community for Chartered Accountancy, Company Secretary and Cost & Management Accountancy students and professionals in India.

How to recognise it

Every request carries this user-agent:

MSC-JobsBot/1.0 (+https://www.mystudentclub.com/bot)

What it does

It reads public job openings on employers' own career pages and on the public job pages of their applicant-tracking systems. It keeps openings in India that CA, CS and CMA students or members can apply for and lists them on My Student Club with a link back to the employer's own job page. Applications always happen on the employer's site.

How it behaves

  • It obeys robots.txt (RFC 9309) and re-reads it every 24 hours.
  • It honours Crawl-delay and otherwise makes no more than about one or two requests a second to a site.
  • It never logs in, never solves CAPTCHAs, never uses proxies to change its address and never disguises itself as a browser user.
  • If a site answers with a rate limit, a firewall block or a challenge page, it stops and tries again another day.
  • It does not crawl sites whose terms prohibit automated access.
  • It fetches a job's full description only when the job is new or has changed.

Opting out

Add a rule for the token MSC-JobsBot to your robots.txt. To exclude the whole site:

User-agent: MSC-JobsBot
Disallow: /

Or email us with the domain you want excluded:

bot@mystudentclub.com

We add it to our exclusion list and stop crawling it within 24 hours, and we remove its listings from My Student Club.
