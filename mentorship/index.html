How it works

Mentorship is part of the MSC Industrial Training Program.

  1. 1

    Pick your mentor

    Browse by company, domain, city and language. See where they did their industrial training and articleship, and what other students say about them.

  2. 2

    They message you within 24 hours

    Your mentor sends a WhatsApp intro, asks for your CV and fixes your first call. You both get an email with each other's details.

  3. 3

    A weekly call, a CV review and a mock interview

    15 minutes every week on how the hunt is going: applications, shortlists, interviews and what to do next. Plus a line-by-line CV review and at least one full mock interview.

  4. 4

    Help until you join

    Offer letter, joining formalities and first-week doubts. Then a joining post on LinkedIn that tags everyone who helped: Padam Bhansali, My Student Club, your mentor and your parents.

Each mentor agrees to these, one by one, before Team MSC approves them.

  • Responds within 4-5 hoursOn WhatsApp. Nothing stays unread at the end of the day.
  • A call every week15 minutes on your stage, your applications and your next steps.
  • Reviews your CVLine by line with the MSC CV checklist, then checks the new version too.
  • Takes a mock interviewAt least one full mock before your real interviews, with honest feedback.

  • Shares the right MSC resourcesGuidebook, interview booklets and CV tools, at the right time.

  • Is an elder brother or sisterPatient, honest and on your side, especially on the bad days.
What your mentor will never do
  • Ask you for money, gifts or paid services
  • Sell you a course or a paid group
  • Move you to another group or community
  • Promise you a job, a referral or a placement
  • Guess. If they are not sure, they check with their senior mentor first

Questions

Frequently asked

Who are the mentors?

Seniors who have done what you are about to do: CA Final students who are doing or have finished industrial training, students who have completed articleship, and qualified CAs. We especially welcome MSC alumni. Each mentor shares their CA journey, scores, CV and LinkedIn with us, completes the mentor training and quiz, and is approved by Team MSC.

Is mentorship included in my program?

Mentorship is part of the MSC Industrial Training Program. Once you are enrolled, log in and pick your mentor. You never pay a mentor anything.

When will my mentor contact me?

Within 24 hours of you picking them, on WhatsApp. You also get an email with your mentor's details, and you can message them first from your My mentor page.

Can I change my mentor?

Yes, once. Use "Need a different mentor?" on your My mentor page and tell us why. Team MSC looks at every request and reassigns you to someone else.

What if my mentor does not respond?

Tell us in your weekly check-in. It takes 30 seconds and goes to Team MSC, not to your mentor. If a mentor is slow or misses calls, we step in, and we reassign you if it does not improve.

Will my mentor get me a job or a referral?

No, and nobody on MSC can promise you a job, a referral or a placement. Your mentor helps you prepare, apply well and keep going, week after week. If anyone asks you for money or promises a job, tell Team MSC.

How are mentors chosen?

Every mentor fills a detailed application: their CA journey, where they did industrial training and articleship, their scores, CV and LinkedIn, and a written answer to a real mentee situation. They agree to every mentor duty one by one, complete the mentor training and pass a quiz. Team MSC then reviews the application. After that, your weekly check-ins and reviews tell us how each mentor is doing.

How much do mentors earn, and who pays them?

Rs 500 per mentee, paid by Team MSC. Students never pay a mentor, and a mentor should never ask you for money.
