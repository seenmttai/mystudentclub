Why a CA resume is different

A generic tool can't read professional CA calibre.

Most resume checkers were trained on standard corporate CVs. They miss what actually decides a CA shortlist — the scale of clients you audited, the compliance standards you applied, and the visual detail of your formatting.

  • Professional Exposure — firm tier, client turnover, and the real scope of your audit, tax and compliance work.
  • Compliance Signals — depth of technical standards (CARO, Ind AS, Income Tax) that generic scanners overlook.
  • Domain Specialisation — direct tax, audit, FP&A or corporate finance, each read on its own terms.

Pro Tips for Accounting & Finance CVs

Quantify Your Impact

Use numbers and percentages to show the scale of your work (e.g., "Audited 20+ clients with ₹500Cr+ revenue")

Technical Terminology

Include relevant standards (Ind AS/IFRS), regulations, and software tools to pass ATS screening

Keep It Concise

Limit your resume to one page with focused, relevant experience and achievements

Highlight Certifications

Showcase additional certifications like NISM, Excel proficiency, or ICAI achievements
