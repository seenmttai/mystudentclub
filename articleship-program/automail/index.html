Automail

Your own sending sheet, in your own Gmail.

  • Pick your cities and it fills in the Big 10 for them, plus firms from the Articleship Cold Mailing List - All Emails.
  • It mails each firm on its own, with the CV for its domain.
  • It follows up three times in the same thread and stops when they reply.

Before you start

  • A laptop with Chrome, signed in to the Gmail you apply from.
  • Your CV and cover letter as PDFs in that account’s Google Drive: one of each for each domain you apply for.
  • Your Gmail signature, set up the way page 1 of Application templates – Day 2 shows. Every mail ends at your name; the signature adds the rest.

Set it up

  1. 1 Make your copy

    Click Make my copy, then Make a copy. Your copy opens in a new tab.

    Signed in to two Google accounts? Open this page in the Chrome profile of the one you apply from.

  2. 2 Start here

    In your copy, open the Automail menu and click Start here. The menu shows a few seconds after the sheet opens.

  3. 3 Allow it

    Click Continue and pick your Gmail. If Google shows “Google hasn’t verified this app”, click Advanced, then Go to Articleship Automail (unsafe): your copy is your own script, which Google hasn’t reviewed. Tick Select all, then Continue.

    Automail needs every box: Gmail to send and spot replies, Drive to attach your CV.

  4. 4 Set up

    Click Start here again and answer the set-up: you, your domain, your CV, your cities, your mail and your practice line.

    Choosing between two domains? Pick both and add a CV and a cover letter for each.

  5. 5 Add firms

    Click Add the Big 10, then Add firms from the cold mailing list. On the last screen, click Start Automail.

    Big 10 portals come in as rows with their link: you apply on the portal yourself.

  6. 6 Test, then send

    On the Status board, pick a mail on each firm you want to write to, and add the line it asks for: for the cold mail, one real detail from the firm’s website. Send a test mail to yourself and read it. Then create drafts, read one and send them.

    Each mail is the Day 2 template, word for word, with your details filled in. The sidebar’s one button always shows your next step.

Each weekday

  • Monday to Friday, 10 am to 6 pm, one mail every 10 minutes, even with your laptop off.
  • First mails to new firms go Monday to Wednesday; a row ticked Opening goes the same day.
  • Three follow-ups, five working days apart, in the same thread.
  • Any reply stops them; so does a bounce or an automatic reply.
Evenings and weekends Found an opening? Tick Opening: Automail makes the draft. Send it by hand from Gmail the same day.

Your daily check

Open Automail from the menu once a day. Answer your replies, then set Interview, Rejected or Offer.

Each follow-up waits for its New line: one true thing you have done since your last mail.

As you reach people at a firm, tick their boxes on its row: Poster messaged, Partner or director, Current article, HR. With Applied, the row shows Fully applied.

Three follow-ups and no reply? The monthly check-in is yours to send, from Gmail.

Remove Automail’s access

  1. In your copy, click Automail, More, Stop Automail.
  2. Open myaccount.google.com/linkedapps (opens in a new tab). Click Access to your Google Account, then Articleship Automail, See details, Remove access, Confirm.
  3. Delete your copy of the sheet if you no longer need it.

Google’s Security Checkup may list Articleship Automail as unverified. That’s your own copy.

One rule

Use Automail only for your own applications, never for client data.
