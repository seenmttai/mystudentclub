How CS training works

  • ICSI calls it long-term practical training. It lasts 21 months and is whole-time.
  • You can start after passing both groups of CS Executive and completing the one-month Executive Development Programme (EDP), or after passing CS Professional.
  • Training must be with an ICSI-registered trainer: a Practising Company Secretary with at least one year of practice, a company that employs a whole-time CS and meets ICSI's size rules, an eligible LLP, or a body such as MCA, SEBI, the stock exchanges, RBI, a PSU or a bank.
  • Register the training with ICSI within 30 days of joining. The first two months are probation.
  • The minimum stipend is ₹5,000 a month nationwide for training registered on or after 1 August 2023. On ICSI's own placement portal in October 2026, the typical offer was about ₹10,000.
  • To become an Associate member (ACS) you need CS Professional, the training (or an exemption), the EDP, the 30-day CLDP and a Training Clearance Certificate.

Summary as of October 2026. Source: ICSI: long-term training. Rules change, so confirm on the ICSI website.

Frequently asked questions

Is CS training the same as CS articleship?

Yes. ICSI's official name is long-term practical training. "Articleship" is a CA term, but most CS students and many employers say CS articleship, CS training or CS internship for the same 21-month programme. Source

When can I start CS training?

After you pass both groups of CS Executive and complete the one-month EDP, or after you pass CS Professional. Passing one Executive group is not enough. Source

What is the minimum stipend for CS trainees?

₹5,000 a month if your training was registered on or after 1 August 2023. It is the same in every city, must be paid into your bank account and is payable during probation too. Many firms and companies pay more. Source

Can I do CS training in a CA firm?

Usually not. ICSI's trainer categories are Practising Company Secretaries, eligible companies and LLPs, and listed bodies, and LLPs registered with ICAI or ICMAI are excluded. Ask the firm whether a PCS will register your training, and confirm with ICSI if unsure. CA firms can still hire semi-qualified and qualified CS for jobs. Source

What is a semi-qualified CS?

Someone who has passed CS Executive or CS Professional but is not yet an ICSI member (ACS). ICSI's own placement portal labels these jobs "Semi Qualified". Typical roles are secretarial executive and compliance executive. Source

I am already working. Can I skip CS training?

An exemption can be claimed only after you pass all groups of CS Professional, and only with specified experience, such as several years in the secretarial department of an eligible company or under an experienced PCS. A fee applies. Source
