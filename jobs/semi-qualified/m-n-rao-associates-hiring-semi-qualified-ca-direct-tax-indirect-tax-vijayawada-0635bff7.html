About the role

Joining by: Immediate

Job Title: Audit Assistant (Fresh Graduate / Post Graduate Track)
Location: Moghalrajpuram, Vijayawada
Employment Type: Full-Time
Firm Name: M N Rao & Associates LLP

About the Firm
M N Rao & Associates LLP is a well-established Chartered Accountancy firm with over 35 years of professional experience in providing comprehensive financial, taxation, audit and advisory services. Over the years, we have expanded our expertise to include audits, taxation, management consultancy, accounting, corporate compliance, Virtual CFO services, US offshoring, and more, positioning ourselves as trusted advisors to businesses across diverse industries.

Job Overview
We are seeking high-merit, detail-oriented graduates / post graduates to join our professional accounting and audit support team. As an Accounts & Audit Assistant, you will work closely with senior auditors and partners to execute day-to-day corporate compliance, book-keeping, and statutory audit support for a diverse portfolio of clients. This role requires sound theoretical accounting knowledge backed by strong execution skills in Tally and Microsoft Excel.
Key Responsibilities

  • Bookkeeping & Accounting: Maintain accurate corporate ledgers, process purchase/sale invoices, and passing journal entries in Tally Prime.
  • Bank Reconciliation: Perform regular Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS) and clear discrepancies in client accounts.
  • Audit Documentation: Assist the audit team with vouching, verification of internal source documents, and preparing audit working papers.
  • Taxation Compliance: Compile data and draft preliminary summaries for GST filing (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B reconciliation with GSTR-2B) and Income Tax returns.
  • Data Management: Organize, clean, and analyse client financial data using Microsoft Excel to generate structural MIS reports.

Required Candidate Profile

  • Education: Fresh B. Com graduate (Regular/Honours/Computer Applications) or post graduate.
  • Technical Software Skills: Hands-on structural training certificate in Tally Prime and Microsoft Excel (Intermediate to Advanced).
  • Excel Capabilities: Must be highly proficient in functions like VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, Pivot Tables, SUMIFS, and Data Validation.

Compensation: ₹20,000 / month
